"I don't have anything to deceive you on, of course I'm not deceiving you.
I'm engaging with you as a human being. Why would I deceive you?"

This week I have decided quite suddenly to write a post about something very
specific as opposed to an update about my life. I went to the theater and
watched Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim's YOU CAN SEE EVERYTHING. I don't
think I have the words to describe how astonished this movie made me feel.
Since the movie was just released, I will not discuss it much further until
I know sufficiently many people have watched it to not worry about spoilers.
Also, I would like to have some more time to sit with the film and see how
my opinions about it change. But I know that if you watch it, you are likely
to have an absolutely amazing time and will be in shock for a good chunk of
the film's running time.

Highly recommend you watch it!

Take care,
